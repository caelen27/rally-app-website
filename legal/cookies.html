Cookie policy

Last updated 5 August 2026 Covers the Firstday website and mobile app

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. It lets a site recognise your browser on a later visit. Similar technologies, including local storage and pixels, do comparable things. This policy covers all of them.

What this website currently sets

Being direct about it: this website sets no cookies of its own. There is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, and no chat widget. Nothing on these pages tracks you or follows you elsewhere.

The contact form does not store anything in your browser either. What you type is sent when you submit it and is covered by the Privacy Policy.

Third parties the pages reach out to

WhoWhyWhat they receive
Google Fonts Serves the Outfit typeface the site is set in. Your IP address, browser, and the page you were on. Google does not set cookies when serving fonts.
Unsplash Serves the photographs in the categories strip on the home page. Your IP address and browser, as with any image loaded from another domain.
Vercel Hosts the site. Standard server request logs. No analytics cookie is enabled.

Both the fonts and the photographs can be served from our own domain instead, which would remove those two third parties entirely. We treat that as a change worth making.

What the app uses

The Firstday app stores information on your device so it can work at all.

These are strictly necessary. Turning them off would stop the app working, so they are not subject to consent, and there is no way to opt out while continuing to use the app.

Listing a trial day is free for Organizations during the pilot, so no payment processor is in use and no payment-related cookies are set. If a paid tier launches later, this page will be updated first.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking technologies anywhere in Firstday.

If that changes

If we add analytics or anything else that is not strictly necessary, we will update this page, ask for your consent before it loads, and give you a way to change your mind. We will not add advertising trackers to a platform used by people under 18.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. The help pages for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge explain how.

Blocking cookies on this website will not affect it, because it does not use any. Blocking them for the app will prevent you signing in.

On a phone you can also reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings, though Firstday does not use it.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no agreed standard for how sites should respond, so we do not act on it specifically. We do not track you across other sites regardless of the setting.
