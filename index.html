The matcher

Matched By What
You Are Good At

Rally opens with a short strengths survey built on the same idea as CliftonStrengths. It reads what you are good at, where you live, and how far you can reasonably travel, then ranks the openings that fit.

A survey, not a test

Around twenty questions sort your strengths into themes. There are no right answers, and you can retake it whenever your interests change.

Questions

If yours is not here, write to caelen@truemeridianai.com and a person will answer.

Is Rally free for youth?

Yes. Making a profile, taking the strengths survey, applying to shifts, and logging hours all cost nothing. Organizations pay the listing fee.

Do the hours count for school?

Rally records each shift and the coordinator confirms it, then you can export the log as a PDF. Requirements differ between boards, so check with your guidance counsellor about what yours accepts before you rely on it.

I am under 16. Can I sign up?

Yes, with a parent or guardian. Anyone under 16 needs a guardian to approve the account, and the guardian is asked to approve each placement before it starts.

What does it cost an organization?

$10 a month keeps your listings up, with no cap on how many you post. If you choose to award a performance bonus to a young person through Rally, we keep 10 percent of that bonus.

Which areas does Rally cover?

The Greater Toronto Area to start, including Toronto, Peel, York, Durham, and Halton.

How does the matcher decide?

It compares the strengths themes from your survey against what an organization says the role needs, then weights the result by travel distance. You see the openings and choose which to apply for. Nothing is booked on your behalf.
