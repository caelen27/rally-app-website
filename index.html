Questions

If yours is not here, write to caelen@truemeridianai.com and a person will answer.

Is Firstday free for youth?

Yes. Making a profile, browsing trial days, and RSVPing all cost nothing, whether you go solo or bring a friend. Organizations list a day for free too, at least during the pilot.

Do I have to bring a friend?

No. Bringing a friend is optional. On a trial day everyone attending is new, so showing up alone is the same as showing up with someone: either way, you are not the only first-timer in the room.

I am under 16. Can I sign up?

Yes, with a parent or guardian. Anyone under 16 needs a guardian to approve the account, and the guardian is asked to approve each RSVP before it is confirmed.

What does it cost an organization?

Nothing right now. Firstday is free for organizations while we run the initial pilot. If a paid tier ever launches, current listings get at least 30 days notice first.

Which areas does Firstday cover?

The Greater Toronto Area to start, including Toronto, Peel, York, Durham, and Halton, beginning with a small pilot group of local organizations.

How does the matcher decide?

It compares what you picked in your interest profile against how an organization describes its trial day, then weights the result by travel distance. You see the matches and choose which to RSVP to. Nothing is booked on your behalf.
